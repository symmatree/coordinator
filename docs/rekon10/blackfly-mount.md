# blackfly mount

## Dimensions

* Camera is 28.8mm tall and same width
* On top are 2 M3 holes, 20.72mm / 25.47mm (23.1mm center), tapped just under 5mm deep
* On bottom are 4 M3 holes on a rectangle. Side to side 20.7mm / 25.3mm (23mm),
  fore/aft 30.7 and 35.3 = 33mm

## Cheese mounting

* 2 pairs of 3/8" holes
* 42.5mm side to side
* 22.125mm fore-aft
